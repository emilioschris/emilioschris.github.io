Language Routes · N 213°

Spanish Trainer

Mexican Spanish, a little every day. Each word climbs from Base Camp to the Summit as you get it right, and slides back down when you don't.

Who you're hearing

Word recordings come from Lingua Libre, a Wikimedia project where volunteers record their own language. Only native speakers from Latin America are used here, Mexican speakers first.

About

I'm from an island in the eastern Mediterranean, where the past sits close to the surface: ruins in the middle of fields, mosaics under village churches, stories older than the roads. Later, life stretched across the Atlantic to a city of rivers and bridges.

Somewhere between the two I picked up a habit of wondering how things came to be: places, languages, animals, whole civilisations. These field notes are where that curiosity goes. I build things with AI to understand the world a little better, and keep them here as I go. Some are finished. Most are still on the trail.

I'm Emilios Christodoulou. I use AI to build this digital garden, and I'm actively fact-checking the results and improving it as I go.
